# RecapRight-Lead-Research-Project
Lead Research for a B2B Saas Brand 

*Sample project: the product, companies and figures are illustrative.*

Lead research for **RecapRight**, a tool that turns meeting recordings into tracked action items.

**Target buyer:** accounting and consulting firms with 10-60 staff.

**Signals searched:** job posts for follow-up coordinators, client reviews on slow follow-through, rapid hiring, partner posts about admin overload.

## Files
- `RecapRight - Lead Research Project` (doc): buyer profile, signals, verification steps, sample leads
- `RecapRight_Sample_Leads.xlsx`: 20 leads with contact role, problem, evidence and priority

## Each lead includes
Contact role, problem, proof, and why now.
